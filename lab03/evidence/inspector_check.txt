TOOL: search_items - Search the catalogue by a word in the item name or form (e.g. 'syrup', 'tablet', 'cough').
TOOL: list_expiring - List items that expire within the next `days` days (including already expired ones).
TOOL: record_sale - ACTION: record a sale and REDUCE stock. Only call when the user clearly asks to sell/record a sale.
